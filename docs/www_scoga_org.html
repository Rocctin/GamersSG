Our Programmes

Esports Academy is a leading platform for developing talent and 21st-century skills. Through structured programmes, we nurture leadership, teamwork, and critical thinking, preparing individuals for careers in and beyond esports.

Our mission is to empower individuals to thrive and foster resilience. We shape future leaders and role models, redefining esports as a platform for growth, opportunity, and meaningful contribution.

Train with the Next Generation

Campus Legends is an annual inter-tertiary esports tournament by SCOGA, designed to engage, educate, and empower youths. Beyond competition, it fosters identity, community, and values like discipline, teamwork, and resilience.

For over 18 years, SCOGA has helped youths build careers in esports. Launched in 2019, Campus Legends provides a national-level platform for student gamers to compete while exploring career pathways in and beyond esports.

Become the Next Legends

Campus Game Fest (CGF) is Singapore’s premier gaming, digital, and youth lifestyle festival, returning in 2024 after a five-year hiatus. As the nation’s longest-running gaming event, CGF unites communities through a shared passion for games, arts, and media.

The festival features esports tournaments, community gaming, cosplay, and interactive workshops, while promoting creativity, innovation, and cyber wellness. CGF remains a cornerstone of Singapore’s youth culture and digital entertainment.
